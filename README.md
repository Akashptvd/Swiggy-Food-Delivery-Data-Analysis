# Swiggy Food Delivery Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of Swiggy food delivery data using Python. The objective is to understand customer ordering patterns, food preferences, restaurant performance, delivery efficiency and customer satisfaction.

The analysis uses 15,000 food delivery records to uncover meaningful insights and provide practical business recommendations.

## Project Objectives
- Analyse customer spending and ordering patterns.
- Identify popular food categories and restaurants.
- Examine order distribution across cities.
- Understand customer ratings and payment preferences.
- Investigate relationships between order value, delivery time and customer satisfaction.
- Compare restaurant and food category performance across cities.
- Identify peak ordering days, hours and time-based food preferences.
- Suggest strategies to improve delivery services, customer satisfaction and business growth.

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset Information
- Total records: 15,000
- Cities: 10
- Restaurants: 40
- Food categories: 10

## Key Findings
- Average order value: ₹611.92.
- North Indian was the most popular food category by order count.
- Punjabi Tadka received the highest number of orders overall.
- Hyderabad recorded the highest number of orders by city.
- Average customer rating was approximately 3.99.
- UPI was the most preferred payment method.
- Approximately 93.89% of orders were delivered.
- Delivery time and customer rating showed a moderate negative correlation of approximately -0.3895.
- Tuesday was the busiest ordering day.
- 8 PM was the busiest ordering hour.
- Nighttime recorded the highest ordering activity.

## Business Recommendations
- Increase delivery partner availability during evening peak hours.
- Strengthen partnerships with high-performing restaurants.
- Promote popular food categories based on city-level preferences.
- Improve delivery coordination to reduce waiting times.
- Monitor customer feedback to improve satisfaction.
- Introduce targeted promotions during less busy periods.

## Project Files

This project demonstrates how exploratory data analysis can help identify customer preferences, evaluate business performance and uncover opportunities to improve food delivery operations.

The insights generated can support better operational decisions, improved customer experiences and sustainable business growth.
